Our Services

Here is a detailed look at what we offer:

1. AC Repair & Installation

Summers can be unbearable without a properly working air conditioner. Our AC repair service covers everything – from regular servicing, gas refilling, cooling issues, and water leakage problems to complete AC installation and uninstallation. We deal with all brands and models, ensuring that your cooling system performs at its best. Our experts also guide you about energy-saving maintenance tips to keep your AC in top condition.

2. Refrigerator Repair

The refrigerator is the heart of every kitchen, and when it fails, your daily life is immediately affected. We offer comprehensive fridge repair services that include fixing cooling issues, replacing compressors, refilling gas, repairing thermostats, and handling unusual noises. Whether you own a single-door, double-door, or side-by-side refrigerator, our technicians provide professional solutions to keep your food fresh and safe.

3. Washing Machine Repair

A washing machine breakdown can cause huge inconvenience, especially in busy households. At Savera Services, we cover semi-automatic, fully automatic, and front-load machines. Our services include fixing drum issues, water leakage, unusual vibrations, display errors, and more. With our expert care, your washing machine will always be ready to handle your laundry with ease.

4. Microwave Oven Service

Microwave ovens make cooking faster and easier. If your microwave is not heating properly, sparking, or showing error codes, our specialists can handle it. We cover all types of microwaves including solo, grill, and convection ovens. From replacing faulty parts to solving electrical issues, we make sure your microwave runs smoothly, helping you save time in your kitchen.

5. TV Repair

Your television is your source of entertainment and relaxation. When it stops working, it can spoil your evenings. Our TV repair service includes fixing LED, LCD, Plasma, and Smart TVs. We deal with screen issues, sound problems, motherboard failures, and software errors. With the latest diagnostic tools, we ensure your TV gives you the best viewing experience again.

6. Water Purifier Installation & Service

Clean water is essential for a healthy life. Our water purifier service includes installation, filter replacement, RO servicing, and complete maintenance. We ensure that you and your family always have access to safe drinking water by checking every part of your purifier thoroughly. Our experts handle all brands and models with equal efficiency.

7. Generator Repair & Maintenance

Power cuts can be frustrating, and that’s when generators save the day. We provide generator servicing and repair to make sure your backup power source is always ready when you need it. From oil changes and filter cleaning to fixing technical faults, our services keep your generator running smoothly for both household and commercial use.

8. Other Appliance Services

Apart from the major appliances, we also offer repair services for other household items. Whether it’s a small kitchen gadget, home electronic device, or another appliance, Savera Services ensures you get professional repair and maintenance under one roof. No matter what the issue is, our skilled technicians are always ready to help.
